# Retail Sales Analytics — Final Case Study
### SWYNEX Data Analyst Internship | Task 4

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end retail sales analytics case study using the Online Retail II dataset. It combines data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business insights, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The main objective is to understand sales performance, identify trends, discover top-performing products, and support data-driven business decisions.

## 2. Problem Statement

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transaction data. Without proper analysis, it can be difficult to identify high-performing products, understand sales trends, and determine which countries contribute the most revenue.

This project addresses these challenges by cleaning retail transaction data, analyzing sales patterns, and presenting key findings through visualizations and a Power BI dashboard.

## 3. Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Online Retail II — 2009–2010
- **Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository
- **Domain:** Retail and E-commerce Analytics
- **Data Type:** Transaction-level retail data

The dataset contains retail transaction information, including invoice numbers, product descriptions, quantities, invoice dates, prices, customer IDs, and countries.

**Dataset source:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii

## 8. Key Business Insights

1. **Monthly performance:** November 2010 recorded the highest monthly sales in the analysis. This period can be investigated further to understand demand patterns and possible seasonal effects.

2. **Country contribution:** The United Kingdom contributed approximately 8.16 million in sales, making it the leading country in the analysis.

3. **Product performance:** WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER was identified as the top-performing product in the analysis. Product-level performance can help businesses plan inventory and promotions.

4. **Interactive reporting:** The Power BI dashboard makes it easier to examine sales trends, compare countries, and explore product performance using filters.

5. **Data quality:** Removing duplicate records and addressing missing values helped prepare the data for more reliable analysis.

## 9. Business Recommendations

- Investigate the factors behind the high sales recorded in November.
- Monitor demand for top-performing products and plan inventory accordingly.
- Analyze sales in other countries to identify opportunities for growth.
- Use monthly and product-level reporting to support promotional planning.
- Maintain regular data quality checks before generating business reports.

These are recommendations based on the observed patterns; further analysis would be needed to confirm their business impact.
